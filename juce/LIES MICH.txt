AXIOM DTQW v1 — fertige Plug-ins (Universal: Apple Silicon + Intel, macOS 10.13+)

Inhalt dieses Ordners:
  • AXIOM DTQW.vst3         VST3 (Cubase, Ableton Live, Reaper, Studio One, Bitwig …)
  • AXIOM DTQW.component    Audio Unit (Logic Pro, GarageBand)
